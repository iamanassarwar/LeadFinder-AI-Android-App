## What's New in v1.0.0

### Core Highlights
* 🔍 **Local Business Discovery & Extraction Engine:** Filter prospects by category and radius, target businesses without websites, and deduplicate records in real-time.
* 📊 **0–100 Lead Intelligence Scoring:** Deterministic scoring algorithm evaluating missing websites, responsiveness, contact channels, and customer review volume.
* 🌐 **Automated Technical Website Audits:** Instant URL diagnostics checking HTTP status, SSL security, mobile viewport configuration, and contact conversion forms.
* 🤖 **Gemini AI Outreach Generator:** Personalized multi-channel pitch generator (Cold Email, WhatsApp, and Direct Messages) with selectable tones and single-tap refinements.
* 💬 **Interactive AI Sales Copilot:** Context-aware assistant to query your saved prospects and get tactical advice on closing deals.
* 📁 **Offline-First CRM & Export:** Native Room database persistence with pipeline status tracking, custom tags, and one-tap CSV/JSON export via the Android Share sheet.

### Technical Specifications
* **Min SDK:** 24 (Android 7.0+) | **Target SDK:** 36
* **UI:** Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3 and Adaptive Navigation (Bottom Bar / Nav Rail)
* **Architecture:** MVVM + Clean Architecture with Coroutines & StateFlow
